Jaipur Engineering College (JEC) Kukas, established in 2000, is a prominent institution in Jaipur, Rajasthan, affiliated with Rajasthan Technical University (RTU), Kota. The college offers a variety of engineering programs, including B.Tech and M.Tech courses, and is known for its state-of-the-art facilities, vibrant campus life, and strong placement cell that connects students with top companies
